Engadin parlour

Arvenstube

Stone-pine panelling from 1908, a tiled stove and the valley's own cooking.

The original parlour of the house, panelled in Swiss stone pine that has darkened to honey over a century. Here we cook the Engadin: capuns wrapped in chard, barley soup with air-dried beef, venison in autumn and nusstorte from the old recipe.

Breakfast is served here until eleven; in the evening, the tiled stove is lit and the room holds forty guests at most.

  • Engadin nusstorte

Chef's counter · 14 seats

Lumen

Fourteen seats around an open hearth and a nine-course menu of the high Alps.

Lumen is a single counter of Arolla pine around an open fire. Chef Lea Caflisch cooks nine courses from within sixty kilometres: lake fish, mountain herbs, cheese from the summer alps and grain from the Val Müstair.

One sitting each evening, Wednesday to Sunday. Wine pairings lean on small Graubünden growers and Valtellina across the pass.

Signature dishes

  • Lake Sils char, juniper and buttermilk
  • Fire-roasted celeriac and alpine cheese
  • Larch-smoked honey and pine ice
