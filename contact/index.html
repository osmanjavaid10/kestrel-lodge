Getting here is half the journey.

Pontresina sits at the head of the Upper Engadin, ten minutes from St. Moritz. However you arrive, the last hour is through some of the most beautiful country in the Alps.

How to arrive

  • By train

    3 h 15 from Zurich

    Change at Chur for the Rhaetian Railway over the Albula line, a UNESCO World Heritage route of viaducts and spiral tunnels. Pontresina station is four minutes from the door; we will meet you.

  • By car

    2 h 45 from Zurich

    A13 to Thusis, then over the Julier pass (open year-round) or by car-train through the Vereina tunnel in winter. Covered parking with charging for electric cars.

  • By air

    10 min from Samedan

    Engadin Airport at Samedan takes private aircraft and helicopters. Zurich and Milan Malpensa are both about three hours by our private transfer.

Before you arrive

When is the lodge open?

Winter season runs from early December to mid-April, summer from early June to late October. We close for maintenance in May and November.

What is included in the room rate?

Breakfast in the Arvenstube, Spa Silenzi access, Wi-Fi, and the Engadin Card, which covers lifts in summer, local buses and trains in the Upper Engadin.

Is there a minimum stay?

Two nights for most arrivals, three nights in February and other peak weeks, and five nights over Christmas and New Year.

Can children stay?

Yes. The Arven Junior Suite, Hearth Loft and Kestrel Penthouse all sleep three or four. The spa is adults-only after 17:00.

Do you allow dogs?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome in the Bernina Room and Hearth Loft for CHF 40 per night, including a bed, bowls and a map of off-lead walks.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival, or 30 days over Christmas and New Year. After that, the first night is charged.

Send us a note

Questions about a stay, a celebration or an event — we reply within a working day.
