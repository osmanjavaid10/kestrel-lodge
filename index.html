The house

A house of larch and stone, kept for the quiet.

Kestrel Lodge has stood above Pontresina since 1908, when a family of mountain guides built a twelve-room inn for the climbers of Piz Bernina. It has grown, slowly, into thirty-eight rooms and suites — and has never stopped feeling like a house.

We keep it simple: honest materials, a kitchen that cooks the valley, and enough space between guests for the mountains to be heard.
