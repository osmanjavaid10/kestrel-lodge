About the room

A quiet corner room framed by one great window and the whole Bernina massif.

The Bernina Room is built around a single deep-set window. Sit in it with a book and the glacier does the rest: blue at dawn, white at noon, rose before dark.

Inside, everything is pared back to stone, larch and wool, with a writing desk facing the peaks and a bathroom tiled in local Andeer granite.

In the room

  • Oiled larch floors with underfloor heating
  • Valais wool bedding and a pillow menu
  • Rain shower and deep stone bath
  • Nespresso and a Swiss tea selection
  • Complimentary minibar of local produce
  • Engadin Card: lifts, buses and funiculars included
  • Window seat with lambskin and reading lamp
