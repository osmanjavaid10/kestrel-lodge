About the room

The top of the house: windows on three sides and a private sauna above the trees.

Named for the falcons that hover over the meadow below, the penthouse occupies the whole top floor. Windows on three sides follow the sun from Piz Bernina to the Albula.

Two bedrooms, a library, a private sauna and a terrace where we will set up a telescope on clear nights.

In the room

  • Oiled larch floors with underfloor heating
  • Valais wool bedding and a pillow menu
  • Rain shower and deep stone bath
  • Nespresso and a Swiss tea selection
  • Complimentary minibar of local produce
  • Engadin Card: lifts, buses and funiculars included
  • Private Finnish sauna
  • Library and telescope terrace
  • Dedicated host
