Body · lead · fine

Mornings begin with larch smoke and the sound of the Flaz river below the terrace.

Each suite faces south across the Engadin, with oiled larch floors, wool from Valais and windows that open onto the mountain air. Breakfast is served until eleven, because the light is worth waiting for.

Rates include breakfast, spa access and the Muottas Muragl funicular.
